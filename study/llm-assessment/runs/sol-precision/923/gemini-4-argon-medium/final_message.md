### Assessment

- **Source (`:module.synapse_workspaces`)**: Declared in `synapses.tf`, instantiating `./modules/analytics/synapse`. When `sql_administrator_login_password` is omitted from settings, it generates a random password and creates four `azurerm_key_vault_secret` resources (`sql_admin_password`, `sql_admin`, `synapse_name`, `synapse_rg_name`) in the Key Vault referenced by `keyvault_id = module.keyvaults[each.value.keyvault_key].id`.
- **Target (`:module.keyvault_access_policies`)**: Declared in `keyvault.tf`, instantiating `./modules/security/keyvault_access_policies` to provision `azurerm_key_vault_access_policy` resources (such as granting `Set`, `Get`, `Delete`, etc. secret permissions to `logged_in_user` and `logged_in_aad_app`, as seen in `examples/data_analytics/200-basic-ml/configuration.tfvars`).
- **Ordering Requirement**: `azurerm_key_vault.keyvault` in `./modules/security/keyvault/keyvault.tf` is created without inline access policies, and `module.keyvaults[...].id` outputs `azurerm_key_vault.keyvault.id` directly. Passing `module.keyvaults[each.value.keyvault_key].id` to `module.synapse_workspaces` only waits for the Key Vault resource itself to be created, not for `module.keyvault_access_policies` to grant secret permissions to the executing principal. Other secret-writing modules (`module.mssql_servers` in `mssql_servers.tf`, `module.virtual_machines` in `virtual_machines.tf`, `module.azuread_applications` and `module.azuread_users` in `azuread.tf`) explicitly declare `depends_on = [module.keyvault_access_policies]`, whereas `module.synapse_workspaces` in `synapses.tf` omits it.
